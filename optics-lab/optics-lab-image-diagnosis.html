Diagnostic reasoning

Why appearance is not enough

Clipping, detector image walk and sample structure can all produce nonuniform fields. Underfilling, aberration and defocus can all broaden features. Low photon counts, electronic noise and motion can all make an image look rough. Change one physical condition at a time and ask whether the symptom follows the predicted mechanism.

Model limitations

This page uses an illustrative synthetic specimen and phenomenological image faults. It teaches diagnostic logic, not quantitative prediction. Real microscopes add sample motion, scattering, aberrations, detector electronics, laser instability, contamination and software configuration errors.
