How to explore
  1. Load the green/red preset and follow excitation downward through position 1 while fluorescence is reflected toward the PMTs.
  2. Notice that position 2 may contain both a 2p excitation blocker and a blue-laser blocker. They reject different unwanted wavelengths.
  3. Remove the 680/SP filter. Visible routing hardly changes, but the protection stack becomes incomplete.
  4. Reverse position 3 or put a green filter at position 4. Compare physical routing with useful signal at each PMT.
  5. Try placing components in deliberately inappropriate positions and use the table to diagnose each error.
Model, limitations & references

Emission spectra are illustrative Gaussians. Dichroics use smooth nominal edges and emission filters use smooth nominal passbands. Real curves depend on coating, incidence angle, polarization, manufacturing tolerance and blocking range. Optical density, PMT quantum efficiency, autofluorescence and absolute throughput are not calculated.

Component names and default combinations follow the supplied Sjöström Lab rig notes. Confirm every installation against current part markings and manufacturer data.
