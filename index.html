How to use these simulations

Choose a simulation to open it in a new tab, then adjust its controls to explore how the model behaves. This menu stays open in its original tab, so you can return here to choose another simulation.

Selected simulations offer adjustable graph text and data downloads. Use a graph’s camera button to save a PNG image for notes or teaching materials.
