How to use this simulation

Adjust means and standard deviations to explore LTP, LTD, and variability. New seed reruns the simulation with fresh random responses. Uncheck Reuse random seed to generate fresh random responses whenever you adjust a response parameter or response count. Reset restores the original experiment.

Responses are Gaussian samples, as in the exam-generation code. Negative responses can occur and are retained. This is a teaching model of response statistics, not a simulation of individual release sites.

The normalized coordinates are mean(after)/mean(before) and [CV(before)/CV(after)]². CV = SDev/mean. Theoretical markers use the entered parameters; observed markers use sample statistics.

The horizontal and diagonal reference lines help explore CV analysis. A location alone does not uniquely identify a mechanism; interpretation depends on release-model assumptions.
